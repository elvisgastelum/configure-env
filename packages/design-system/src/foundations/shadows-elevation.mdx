# Shadows & Elevation

Shadows are soft and used sparingly: Tailwind's scale for lift, a brand tint (`shadow-primary/…`) for anything that belongs to the brand, and two named shadows for one-off highlights.

<Callout>
  Samples follow the Theme switch in the toolbar. Brand tints (`primary/…`) are
  shown on `shadow-lg`, and the header hairline on `shadow-sm`, because a tint
  needs a shadow to color.
</Callout>

## Stacking layers

Only four z-index steps are in use. Decorations sit behind the page (`-z-10` inside an `isolate` parent), and only the header and overlays rise above it.

<ZIndexStack entries={Z_INDEX} />

<Callout title="Keep decorations contained">
  A `-z-10` layer needs a parent that is `relative isolate`; otherwise it can
  slip behind the page background and disappear.
</Callout>
