# Effects

Soft brand glows behind page tops and a light frost on bars that float over content. Effects are decorative only: they are hidden from assistive technology and never carry meaning.

<Callout>
  Glows mix the live `--brand` and `--primary` with transparency, so they follow
  the Theme switch in the toolbar.
</Callout>

## Radial glows

Each glow is a `closest-side` radial gradient from a brand tint to transparent. Shown here without the blur the site adds on top (`blur-2xl` / `blur-3xl`).

<GlowGrid entries={GLOWS} />

## PageGlow

The `PageGlow` atom places the page glow behind a page's heading, plus a smaller one at the bottom right from `md`. Its parent must be `relative isolate`.
