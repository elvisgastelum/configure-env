# Motion

Motion explains what changed and where things came from, then gets out of the way. One curve, short durations, and only transform and opacity, so nothing reflows and nothing waits.

<Callout title="Reduced motion">
  Every story is wrapped in `SiteMotionConfig` (`reducedMotion="user"`). With
  the OS "reduce motion" setting on, reveals render in place, scroll-linked
  effects stay still, the spotlight is off, and the CSS transitions and
  keyframes marked `motion-safe:` do not run.
</Callout>

## Easing

`EASE_OUT` (ease-out-quint) starts quick and settles softly. The same curve is `--ease-site` in CSS. Tailwind's own `ease-out` is still used by some transitions; it is gentler at the start and is a known inconsistency, kept because unifying it is a visible change.

## Page view transitions

Page changes run as view transitions: a 0.3s crossfade everywhere, and a directional slide where transition types are supported (the old page leaves in 0.25s, the new one arrives in 0.4s, shifted by ±2rem). They are off under reduced motion. They need real navigation, so try them on the site.

Use **Replay** to play an entrance again. Open a story on its own (from the sidebar) to see it full size.

### Reveal

Fades in with a 24px slide from its `direction` when it scrolls into view, once. Reduced motion: rendered in place.

### RevealGroup

Reveals its items one `STAGGER` (0.08s) apart. Reduced motion: all items in place.

### MaskedWords

Words rise out of a clipping mask, one `WORD_STAGGER` apart; it needs a parent (here a `RevealGroup`) that drives hidden to visible. Reduced motion: words in place.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.MaskedWordsHeadline} />

### PageEntrance

A short fade and 8px rise when a page mounts. Reduced motion: off.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.PageEntranceDemo} />

### SlideIn

Paired cards slide in from their sides as the page scrolls and play back when it scrolls up. Active only from 768px wide; on one column and under reduced motion the cards stay in place. Scroll this page past the demo, or open the story on its own.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.SlideInPair} />

### CardSpotlight

A brand glow that follows the pointer across a card. Its host card needs `relative isolate overflow-hidden group/card`. Mouse or trackpad only; off under reduced motion.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.CardSpotlightDemo} />

### ActiveIndicator

The pill glides to the selected item through a shared `layoutId`. Its button needs `relative isolate`. Reduced motion: it jumps.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.ActiveIndicatorNav} />

### ScrollProgress

A 2px bar fixed along the top of the window that fills with the scroll, smoothed by a spring. On this page it tracks the whole docs page; open the story on its own for a clean view. Reduced motion: it tracks the scroll exactly, without the spring.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.ScrollProgressDemo} />

### useScrolled

`useScrolled(threshold = 8)` is true once the window scrolls past `threshold` px, and re-renders only when that flips. Use it for a header that gains a border or shadow on scroll.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.UseScrolledDemo} />

### Hero glow keyframe

`motion-safe:animate-hero-glow`: the glow drifts down and grows into place over 2.4s on load; the side glow starts 300ms later. Reduced motion: shown in place.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.HeroGlow} />

### Hover and press

`ctaClassName` lifts a CTA 2px on hover and presses it to 98% when tapped; `INTERACTIVE_CARD` lifts a card 4px with a brand shadow and ring; arrows nudge 2px. Reduced motion: colors and shadows still change, nothing moves.
